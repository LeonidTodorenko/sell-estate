# OwnersClub customer knowledge

## 1. Platform and shares
OwnersClub lets investors participate in properties by acquiring investment shares. The primary share price is the property price divided by its total shares. The cost of a primary purchase depends on the number of shares requested and the property's current share price. Check the property card for price and availability. A pending reservation and a confirmed investment are different. Avoid assertions about legal title, guarantees, liquidity, taxes or investment suitability.

## 2. Properties, funding stages and payment plans
Use the property catalog and property details to find available shares, photos and other materials when supplied. The property payment plan shows dated funding stages and the amounts required and funded. Funding progress concerns the property stage, not a schedule of personal instalments owed by the investor. The first application window opens on the date shown for that property. Reload the plan if the selected stage changes before confirmation. Do not infer current prices, availability, stage dates or coverage.

## 3. Investment applications
Select the property and number of shares, review the amount and confirm in the app. A primary purchase requires sufficient wallet funds and available shares. During the first active stage the system debits the wallet and reserves shares in a pending application. When direct allocation applies outside that first active stage, a successful purchase creates an investment immediately. A pending application may be accepted, partially accepted, rejected or moved to an available next stage. Partial acceptance allocates the approved shares and returns the unused portion of the original reservation to the wallet. Rejection releases the reserved shares and refunds the reservation. Moving the reservation carries the same funds forward without a second debit. Check the application history and messages for the actual decision.

## 4. Rent, wallet and financial operations
When a rental payout is made, the gross distribution is proportional to the investor's recorded shares relative to the property's total shares. The applicable fee is deducted before the net amount is credited to the wallet. Use rental income history to check actual payouts and the wallet or transaction history to review credits and debits. An advertised estimate, where available, is distinct from a payout. Use the options displayed in Add Funds and review the charge and wallet credit amounts and currencies. A pending payment is not a confirmed credit. For eligible manual test payments, evidence can be attached to the open request. For withdrawals, follow the options shown in the wallet and check the request status. Demo activity is simulated. Do not quote a payout date, available payment method, bank processing time or live balance from this guide.

## 5. Marketplace
The Marketplace lets investors list eligible shares, browse active offers, place bids and buy at a listed buyout price when offered. Listed shares are held for the offer. A bid is a proposal and does not by itself complete a purchase. The app checks the starting price, share quantity and wallet balance for a bid. An offer buyout buys from the listing at its stated buyout price. Selling back to the platform is a separate operation for the investor's shares in a property. It requires a configured property buyback price and available platform funds, and is unavailable in Demo. Do not promise a buyer, execution of a bid or the ability to exit. Check the listing and confirmation for current terms.

## 6. Owners Club, fees and referrals
Club status is based on assets as calculated in Club, with tiers Explorer, Expert, Visionary and VIP. The applicable rental fee depends on the tier and whether an active referral relationship applies. A positive trading profit can be subject to a fee. Do not apply the rental rate to every operation or assume a listing cancellation is free. The Club screen shows the member's tier, rates and invitation eligibility. An active referral can affect fees, and an eligible inviter may receive a share of the fee during the reward term. Use the invitation flow and invitation history to check availability and status. Do not assume eligibility, earnings or a currently applicable rate.

## 7. Registration, identity verification and security
Register using the registration form and follow the email confirmation flow. Use the profile to review settings and submit identity verification documents through the app's verification flow. Submitting documents puts them under review. Check the status in the account. Some financial operations ask for a PIN or password in the app. Use the app's password recovery flow or support for access problems. Never request or accept passwords, PINs, identity documents or verification codes in chat.

## 8. FAQ and answer limits
For a starting amount, explain how the property's share price works and direct the investor to the property card. For a pending application or payment, explain the difference between reserved or pending and completed, and direct the investor to the relevant history. For rent, explain the distribution and where to check actual payouts. Use prior turns to resolve short follow-ups and ask one focused question if the object or operation is unclear. For account-specific amounts, dates, conditions, or decisions, direct the investor to the relevant screen or support. Explain confirmed product processes without making personalized recommendations, forecasting returns or promising performance. You cannot access private account records or take actions. Present answers in the requested session language and keep internal references out of customer-facing prose.
